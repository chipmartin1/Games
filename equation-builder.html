Equation Builder

Solve 20 two-step equations by building the solution one step at a time. For each problem, drag boxes into the work area to show what you do, what the new equation is, and a check that proves your answer.

How it works

Work backward: undo the last operation first. Do the same thing to both sides. When the whole group in parentheses is being multiplied, undo that multiplication first.

Drag a box into a slot, or tap a box to drop it into the next empty slot. Tap a placed box to send it back. Some boxes are decoys, so you will not use all of them. Blue boxes describe a move. Yellow boxes are equations.
